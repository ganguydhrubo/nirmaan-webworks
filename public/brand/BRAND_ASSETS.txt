Usage
- SVG: preferred for website UI, header, footer, favicon source, and responsive scaling.
- PNG: preferred when transparency is required.
- JPG: only for fixed dark-background placements where transparency is unnecessary.
- Do not stretch, skew, recolor, rotate, add shadows, or recreate the logo with CSS/text.
- Keep clear space around the mark and preserve aspect ratio.
