# Completing the 15-category demo pool

Research date: 15 September 2026. This extends RESEARCH.md Part 3; original scores remain historical judgements. All six remaining categories are retained. Method: fresh search and direct first-party page review of navigation, content hierarchy, buying decisions and interactions. These are design observations, not conversion studies. India-specific implications are inferences from reference sites, not population-level findings. Reference assets and copy are not reused.

## 10. Professional services — Meridian Advisory, Ahmedabad (111/190)

**Research.** [Trilegal](https://trilegal.com/) separates industries, practices, people and knowledge. Its expertise taxonomy is useful; awards and regulated legal-practice claims are not appropriate to copy. [BCG](https://www.bcg.com/) organises expertise around business questions and editorial insights. Local implication: help an owner identify scope before enquiring. Meridian is a business advisory concept, not a purported chartered accountant or law practice; no invented licences or deal histories.

**Design brief.** Deep forest and pistachio, monumental restrained type and Instrument Serif italics make words the hero. A crosshair and ruled baseline give the composition direction. The signature interaction is a native exclusive accordion that unfolds a starting brief from a founder’s question and carries the context into a real website enquiry. A sticky method heading accompanies three progressively drawn chapter rules. Trilegal informs practice structure; BCG informs question framing. Content and the brief selector work without JavaScript.

**Build.** `/demos/meridian-advisory`; Zod-validated practices, both registries, industry content, preview card and smoke entry. Shared new-demo mechanics are scoped to `.studio-demo`; no marketing palette changes or animation dependencies. Full verification follows in `DEMO-EXPANSION-VERIFICATION.md`.


## 11. Fitness — Repwork Studio, Bengaluru (110/190)

**Research.** [cult.fit](https://www.cult.fit/) and its [first-party workout introduction](https://blog.cult.fit/posts/how-we-design-our-workouts) make coached formats and scalable sessions central. [1Rebel concepts](https://www.1rebel.com/en-gb/concepts) separates workouts by experience, with strong naming and explicit formats. Local inference: class time, first-visit comfort and membership validity are useful conversion information.

**Design brief.** Carbon-black, electric lime, enormous compressed-feeling DM Sans headlines and a full-bleed barbell portrait. The signature class finder filters an eight-session week by day and format, including empty and reset states. A three-stage first-visit story advances through ruled markers alongside a sticky headline; no fictional body transformations. Native session details remain usable with JS off. cult.fit informs the timetable and onboarding; 1Rebel informs format naming and energy.

**Build.** `/demos/repwork-studio`, Zod schedule, both registries, industry content, preview and smoke entry. CSS motion only, reduced-motion overrides; filters appear only once listeners exist. Reuses existing locally hosted OFL DM Sans.
